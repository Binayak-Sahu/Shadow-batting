How to set up

    
    
    
      
  1. Phone upright on a box/chair, lens about 1 m high, 3–4 m away, exactly where the bowler would be.
    2. 
      
  2. Point it straight at you and keep it level (not tilted up or down).
    3. 
      
  3. Stand in the middle of the picture, head to feet visible, with a little space below your feet.
    4. 
      
  4. Tap Start, walk back, take your stance and hold still 2 s to calibrate.
    5. 
      
  5. Play the shot, hold the finish ~½ s, then go back to your stance. Reps are counted automatically.
    6. 
      
  6. Your front (left) leg is yellow. The labels on the screen edges show which side is OFF and LEG (the picture is mirrored like a mirror).

Good light

    
Light on you (not behind you), plain background, shorts or fitted trousers and visible shoes help the tracking. Measure in daylight — evening shadows make feet jumpy.
